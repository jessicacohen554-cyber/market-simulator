==============================================================================

  class_hourly.mw. For a CHP class the model's LP capacity is only the
  GRID-FACING share: fleet/assembly.py builds grid_cap = nameplate x
  (1 - btm), and the behind-the-meter host self-supply is never created
  as a Generator (K4, verified in code). The basis-matched comparable is
  therefore CAMPD gross x parasitic x (1 - btm).

  bound. For CHP that ceiling is grid_cap = nameplate x (1 - btm).
