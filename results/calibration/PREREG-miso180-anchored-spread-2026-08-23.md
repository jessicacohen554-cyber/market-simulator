## 2. THE ANCHOR IDENTIFICATION — adjudicated and frozen NOW, before any measurement

The one open design question the charter leaves is the anchor rank. It must
be structural/measured (rules 23/13), never residual-fitted: **an anchor
swept against C3a is the fitted-adder rule-1 violation and is a KILL, not an
option.** The three chartered candidates, adjudicated ex ante:

* **(i) The model/book distribution crossing rank on the frozen miso-179
  H\* constructions — CHOSEN.** The crossing of the two K-PRE-a curves
  (model affected-stack `mc_base` vs BOOK-ELIG base level, both cap-weighted
  $/MWh pooled on H\*) is the empirical boundary between the measured
  over-region (body, where raising is forbidden) and the measured
  under-region (tail, where the residual object lives). It is the unique
  rank that makes a raise-only graft both (a) consistent with the body-over
  measurement — nothing below it is touched — and (b) gap-covering — every
  rank the measurement says is under sits above it. Its identification path
  contains **inputs only**: the H\* hour set (demand-side, frozen by
  miso-179 before this lane's target existed), the model's input offer
  surface `mc_base` (no solve output), and the measured book (no LMP, no
  residual, no award column). Nothing in the path moves when the model's
  price error moves.
* **(ii) A book-side-only shape knee of Q (curvature) — REJECTED.** A knee
  of the book's own quantile function carries no information about where the
  MODEL diverges from the book — the phenomenon is a two-distribution gap,
  not a book-alone feature. A knee below the crossing would command raises
  in ranks the measurement says are already OVER (the K-PRE-c body lesson at
  partial strength), and the curvature of an empirical 199-point quantile
  vector is estimator-fragile: it requires smoothing/scale choices this
  prereg would have to invent — unfrozen researcher DOF with no market
  driver behind them (rule 23 smell).
* **(iii) A conduct-boundary anchor (the rank where the book's above-P_mod
  eligible mass begins) — REJECTED.** It conditions the anchor on the
  model's clearing PRICE — an endogenous solve OUTPUT (the keeper's
  committed hourlies). The anchor would drift with every mechanism change
  and is self-referential under the lever's own action (raising offers moves
  P_mod, which moves the anchor that defined the raise). It also mixes
  price space into an offer-rank identification and needs an unfrozen
  per-hour→scalar aggregation. Candidate (i) uses only input-side offer
  surfaces and needs neither.
* **(i-b) A refetch-free variant of (i) — crossing the model's H\* curve
  against the committed POOLED vector × G_ref — CONSIDERED AND REJECTED.**
  The pooled vector averages all JJA hours of three years, while the H\*
  book is conditioned on the binding window (the committed record shows the
  conditioning is large: eligible p95 127.22 on H\* vs ≈ 24.46 MMBtu/MWh ×
  G_ref ≈ $86 pooled). Crossing a tightness-conditioned model curve against
  an unconditional book curve locates the conditioning mismatch, not the
  conduct gap. The boundary must come from like-conditioned curves; the
  refetch grant exists for exactly this.

> `D(p) = Q_mod_H*(p) − Q_book_elig_H*(p)`;  **r_anchor = max{p ∈ GRID :
> D(p) ≥ 0}** — the last rank at which the model's stack is at or above the
> eligible book on the binding window.
