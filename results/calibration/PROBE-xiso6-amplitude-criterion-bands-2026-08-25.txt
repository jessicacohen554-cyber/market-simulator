D. THE CANCELLATION TRAP — what an amplitude repair does to C3a
==============================================================================
  Accounting decomposition, NOT a mechanism. Hour-of-day profile split into the 12
  hours the MEASURED profile ranks highest / lowest. 'peak-only' repairs the peak half
  to measured and leaves the trough; 'trough-only' is the mirror. Any successor that
  closes amplitude lands BETWEEN these two level errors (or outside, if it overshoots).
  C3a band at HEAD: PASS iff |level err| <= 10% (target and commercial are COINCIDENT, so
  there is no commercial-band caveat for C3a — it is PASS or FAIL).
