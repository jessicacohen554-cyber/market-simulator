## 0. Bottom line

**The three-zone arm was killed, as pre-registered, on legs (i), (ii) and (iii) of the STOP gate.** With
`SPP-Oklahoma` carved out of the P1 South and the two chain links rated by the SPP-53 FCITC construction
on a three-point spread — **N↔OK 6,500 MW, OK↔S 6,700 MW** — the 2025 solve on keeper-2's recipe has
**neither link live**: N↔OK sits at its bound 215 h (2.5 %, below the 5 % liveness floor), OK↔S
never. The Oklahoma bubble therefore traps nothing (re-curtailment 0.00 %), the S−OK spread the link
would have carried is identically zero against a measured +$11.29, and the model's negative-price hours
fall from the keeper's 6 to **0** against ~1,000 measured. The arm did not merely miss its gates: it
**removed the one binding structure the keeper has** (the 3,400 MW N↔S link, at bound 1,573 / 228 h in
keeper-2's 2025) and added none. The full span was not spent, nothing was registered, and the topology
was **not landed** — the solve path on this branch is restored byte-identical to keeper-2's two zones
(§7).

**Owner question, answered (asked in-session, verbatim: *"Is this a recommended keeper candidate? If so
plz promote. If structural integrity improves but gates regress that may still be a keeper"*): NO.**
The standing rule protects a candidate whose structure improves while a residual worsens. Here the
structure regressed — two inert pipes replaced one live one — so there is nothing the rule can protect.

**What the screen's own flows say about the re-issue, reported not re-cut (§5.3):** at the
corridor-only rating SPP-53 already derived (**3,400 MW**) the N↔OK link would sit at or over its bound
**2,065 h, 1,931 N→OK / 134 OK→N** — live, and in the measured-dominant direction; at ~3,400 MW the
OK↔S link 622 h, 603 of them S→OK. The construction that killed the arm is the **union** rule of the
PRECOMMIT §3.2 — pooling the Oklahoma-internal group into the N↔OK set — whose flaw the config comment
stated before the solve: western-Oklahoma delivery elements (Gracemont–Anadarko, Cimarron, Woodward)
identify as N→OK-loaded on the hub-pair regression because their binding makes the OKC hub dearer, and
the same elements identify as S→OK-loaded on the second link. A double attribution that inflates both
pipes past the flows the bubbles produce. **The recommendation to the desk is a re-issue, not a
re-cut in this lane** (§8).

Everything the re-issue needs is landed or in this branch's history: the CSWS split (a measured
