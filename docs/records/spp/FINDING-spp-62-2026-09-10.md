## 2. The seam, and rule 23 `[R-FROZEN-DERIVE]`

each solved year's own `vintage_<year>/` release, behind a new `--census-vintage` argument whose
**default is `None` — the canonical snapshot alone, today's construction byte-for-byte.**

the **DERIVE'S OWN INPUT** — its plant census read an EIA-860 registry measured wrong for the
solved year, and the correct release was already committed on disk — **not a residual that moved.**
Three independent facts make it stand on its own:

1. **The rank is not a choice.** EIA-923 reports `SUB` for 6193 in 2023 and 2024;
   `_COAL_SOURCE_TO_SUPPLY['SUB'] == 'prb'`; the benchmark's own resolver maps `SUB` → `COAL_PRB`.
   The derive reaches `prb` at **100 %** of ranked weight (`n_ranks=1`), by the same arithmetic that
   ranks the other 29 plants. There is no free value to set.
2. **It is inert for the keeper**, so it cannot have been selected on the keeper's residual.
3. **It moves the target residual the WRONG way.** A change fitted to a residual does not do that.

The `--year` span is untouched, so the **pre-patch script at HEAD reproduces the committed CSV
byte-identically** (md5 `5a71bd95bf10a732af980086e541ffa3`). That anchor is what makes §3 exact
rather than approximate.
