**(3) The reason the screen finds so little is a fact about the instrument, not

*the screen works on what the book carries; the book carries only part of

**(4) The correction the screen CAN make is small, and that is worth more than

## 7. Rule-13 admissibility, recorded even though no arm follows

The screen classifies rows of an **input corpus** for the purpose of
measurement; nothing it produces enters the LP and no `ScenarioConfig` field is
added, so rule 13 `[R-MEASURED]` is not engaged by the screen itself. It is
engaged by anything a successor builds on it, and PREREG §9's adjudication
stands unchanged: a measured same-year offer curve pinned into the backcast is a
measured **outcome** overlay with no forward analogue — forbidden as
methodology, admissible at most as an explicitly-labelled default-**off**
diagnostic probe. The only admissible form is a parameter derived from
**multi-year** offer history conditioned on drivers that exist in a forecast
year (position in the stack / headroom, load percentile, gas price), which would
regenerate forward and respond to changed conditions. Any successor must state
how it **replaces or subsumes** `gas_offer_margin` (armed, cell `K`, same offer
path) per rule 19 `[R-ONE-MECH]` — never stacks.

## 8. The successors, named and NOT opened

**(a) The level question's blocker is now a DATA question, not a method
question — and there is a cheaper way around it that needs no screen at all.**
The universe crossing is not fixed by subtracting VRE from the corpus (most of
MISO's VRE is not in the corpus to subtract). It is fixed by **adding the
model's own VRE to the model's curve**: the keeper's wind and solar are LP
decision variables at MC = 0, and a supply curve that includes them at their own
offer price is the like-for-like against a book that carries its VRE at ≤ $0.
That symmetrises the universe on the **model** side, costs no new data, and
dissolves the blocker the screen could not. Its own residual asymmetry must be
stated when it is built: the model's **17.2 GW of import tranches** still have
no analogue in a book of MISO-internal resources, and they are priced in the
*body* of the curve rather than at its foot. **Named, not opened.**

**(b) The miso-145 shape object is UNAFFECTED and remains the live successor.**
The *missing offer wall* rests only on band-restricted, price-identified
statistics — MW priced inside a stated band on both sides — which a population
sitting far below the band cannot move. Nothing measured here touches it: the
model still carries **5.698–6.038 GW** priced between its own clearing and the
hour's actual where the real book carries ≈ 0, and the real curve still rises
**$73.46/MWh per GW** above its clearing against the model's **$1.496**. The
named-not-opened MISO `measured_offer_surface` (position-conditioned, not
class-conditioned, replacing or subsuming `gas_offer_margin`) is unchanged and
still an owner decision (rules 19/24).

**(c) If the level question is to be answered from this corpus rather than
around it**, the missing input is an identification of MISO's
intermittent/dispatchable-intermittent resource population that does not depend
on declaration dynamics — a registry, a resource-type posting, or an hourly
wind/solar forecast artifact joinable to the masked codes. Absent that, **the
answer is (a): fix the universe on the model side.**
