## 5. EXTERNAL RESEARCH

**Confirmed from primary sources**
- **What the FERC-714 system lambda is.** FERC Form 714 Part II Sch. 6: "the fuel cost for a set of on-line and loaded thermal generating units … is minimum when each unit is loaded and operating at the same incremental fuel cost … equal to the system demand plus the net of interchange … This single incremental cost of energy is the system lambda" (excluding transmission losses, hourly, 1 a.m. Jan 1 start) — https://www.ferc.gov/sites/default/files/2020-06/sample-form.pdf (text as summarised by search; PDF text layer not extractable here). Southern files a fixed UTC−6 clock, 24 values every day, 0 gaps (`data/raw/ferc-714/README.md`).
- **How Southern computes its dispatch cost (the λ it files).** Intercompany Interchange Contract, FERC Rate Schedule 138, Art. III (fetched and text-extracted this session): §3.1 "System generating resources are dispatched based on **marginal replacement fuel cost**, variable O&M, in-plant fuel handling costs, emission allowance replacement costs, **compensation for transmission losses**, and other such energy related costs … **A purchase is recognized in economic dispatch on the basis of its energy cost**"; §3.2 the hourly rate "is based on the variable dispatch cost of the incremental resource(s)"; §3.6 "**Marginal replacement fuel costs for coal** are determined at least monthly … and reflect the **current market price for additional coal** … For natural gas or oil-fired units … updated each business day based upon **next day market prices**"; §3.5 blended cost = weighted average of receipts (used only for certain legacy contracts, §3.3.1). Source: https://www.southerncompany.com/content/dam/southerncompany/pdfs/about/suppliers/energy-auction/auction-rules-/Intercompany-Interchange-Contract-5-18-07.pdf. Consequence: the model prices coal at EIA-923 blended receipts (soco-94 §2 "coal is exact") — that is the §3.5 basis, not the §3.6 basis λ uses. Gas is priced at F923 delivered level + HH daily shape, which approximates "next day market prices" only up to the SE basis (owner closed).
- **Loss term.** The IIC's Art. IV capacity formula carries "%TL = Six (6) percent incremental transmission losses" (§4.2.8); the hourly dispatch PF is not published. Southern's Schedule VI formula with `× TPF` is quoted in `FINDING-soco-82` §3.
- **2022 coal did NOT surge at SOCO.** Southern system mix 2021 ≈ 21 % coal / 48 % gas → 2022 20 % coal / 50 % gas; total 179 → 186 TWh; 2022 fuel cost 3.67 ¢/kWh coal vs 5.29 ¢ gas — Southern Company 2022 Annual Report, https://s27.q4cdn.com/273397814/files/doc_financials/2023/ar/Southern-Company-2022-Annual-Report.pdf. The repo's bench agrees (coal 48.8 → 45.5 TWh, gas 121.3 → 131.1). **The model is the one that surges** (coal 53.0 in 2022, +7.5 TWh over actual) because it prices coal at blended receipts while 2022 spot coal was $100–170/ton (CAPP $106/ton week of 2022-04-01, record ~$168 mid-2022; ILB $200/st July 2022) — https://www.coalage.com/departments/breaking-news/us-spot-coal-prices-jump-to-more-than-100-ton/ , https://www.argusmedia.com/es/news-and-insights/latest-market-news/2265167-prb-illinois-basin-coal-prices-reach-record-highs. Georgia Power under-recovered ~$2.1 B of 2022 fuel (coal "rose even more steeply" than gas) — https://georgiarecorder.com/2023/05/16/state-regulators-approve-12-georgia-power-rate-hike-to-start-this-summer/. This is the evidence behind L1.
- **2019 coal.** Scherer CF 40 % in 2019 (51 % in 2018); Southeast coal generation −18 % in 2019 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plant_Scherer , https://www.eia.gov/todayinenergy/detail.php?id=43675. Southeast utilities "uneconomically committed coal at a $5.3 billion price tag for customers" 2015–2022, over a third of the national total — RMI Utility Transition Hub, https://utilitytransitionhub.rmi.org/economic-dispatch/ ; Sierra Club "Other People's Money" (Oct 2019) documents systematic out-of-merit coal at vertically integrated utilities — https://www.sierraclub.org/sites/www.sierraclub.org/files/Other%20Peoples%20Money%20Non-Economic%20Dispatch%20Paper%20Oct%202019.pdf. This corroborates soco-82's measurement (Barry/Wansley out of merit vs Southern's own λ) and supports the ledger: the driver is utility conduct, not a cost.
- **Elliott.** Southern declared an energy emergency but shed no firm load; TVA shed up to 3,000 MW, Duke/Santee Cooper shed — FERC–NERC inquiry https://www.ferc.gov/sites/default/files/2024-02/24_Winter-Storm_Elliot_0207_UPDATE.pdf (soco-97 used it: 913 MW + 1,390 MW Southern unplanned outages). λ hit $1,657 (2022 max, `data/raw/ferc-714/README.md`), which under IIC §3.1 is the energy cost of emergency purchases, not a unit cost.
