How established models treat the phenomena behind the misses (my inference from the literature found; none of these transfers a number):
- **Self-commitment / minimum generation**: NREL's ReEDS→PLEXOS pipeline treats nuclear/biopower as must-run by user list and adds min-stable-level, min up/down and start costs only in PLEXOS (NREL TP-6A40-80669, https://docs.nrel.gov/docs/fy22osti/80669.pdf). A pure LP without commitment variables is the known weak form for coal/CC conduct; the industry answer is MIP UC, which rule-of-stack forbids here. SPP-102's relaxed-UC posture is the closest admissible analogue and it is already built (R, unpaired).
- **Scarcity tail**: ISO ORDC/VRL mechanisms are 5-minute, co-optimised reserve objects (OSTI 2424806 survey, https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/2424806). SPP-55 built the published CR demand curve and measured it inert because the hourly LP has 3–17× the reserve headroom in SPP's shortage hours. This is the model-class gap the rubric names, not a missing parameter.
- **Coal supply shocks**: production-cost practice (Aurora/PLEXOS) represents 2022 rail constraints as fuel-availability limits or opportunity-cost adders set by the user; the MMU did the same through RR502. The model has the stock data but no delivery-reliability driver (N2).
