## 1. Supply-consistent demand — STOPPED

**Built:** `frontend/data/backcast/bench/CAISO/{2019,2020,2021}.json.gz` by the new
`scripts/data/build_bench_part_zero_lp.py`. It uses a fleet-only rebuild of the keeper recipe plus R-CAISO's §1
input flags and a `caiso_supply_consistent_demand=false` scaffold, with the solve's own `_dispatch_frame` at
0 MW, `build_benchmark_frames` and `_btm_frame`, and the canonical render. **Validation:** at `2a20e9d9` (the
commit that last wrote the committed parts) it reproduces CAISO 2022/2023/2024 **key-for-key with no
difference**. 2025 differs only because `regen_caiso_bench_cems.py` retrofits that preliminary-923 year. At HEAD,
2022 differs only in the CC_CHP/CT_CHP BTM subtrahend (−0.0045 / −0.0379 TWh). That comes from code/data drift
since 2026-09-22, not from the scaffold.

**Guard pre-registered before the derive** (the G-DEMAND construction caiso-262 used): wedge band
[−1.500, +19.175] TWh on the repaired 930 identity. The resulting windows were 2019 (189.4, 210.1),
2020 (190.4, 211.1) and 2021 (194.1, 214.8).

**Result:** the derive failed its guard in every year, so nothing was written:

| year | derived | identity | wedge | NG cell | CEMS gas grid | cogen | fold-in |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2019 | 217.280 | 208.73 | −8.55 | 65.251 | 50.842 | 8.197 | 14.763 |
| 2020 | 215.623 | 209.60 | −6.03 | 73.736 | 56.817 | 7.876 | 15.070 |
| 2021 | 217.389 | 213.57 | −3.82 | 79.286 | 61.121 | 7.507 | 14.483 |
| 2022 (HEAD, reference) | 219.275 | 218.81 | −0.47 | 82.796 | 63.042 | 6.827 | 13.393 |

**Root-cause evidence (measured, not tuned).** The guard assumes that the EIA-930 CISO `NG: NG` cell folds in
geothermal and biomass. CAISO's own Outlook fuel mix says it does not. The 930 NG cell equals Outlook
`natural_gas` **alone**: 65.15 vs 64.95 (2019), 73.69 vs 73.82 (2020), 79.23 vs 79.20 (2021), and 5.535 vs
5.535 (Jan 2022) and 8.394 vs 8.394 (Jul 2022). Outlook geothermal + biomass + biogas (≈12.4 TWh/yr) appear in
no 930 cell (GEO = 0, OTH ≈ 0). That fits the chronic Demand-vs-NetGen identity gap.

The derive formula itself still adds geo/bio exactly once in these years. The fold-in term re-adds what
`Net generation` never contained. The failure is in the guard's premise, which was built on the 2023–25
wedge. The 2022 "agreement" (wedge −0.73) is a coincidence of a 12.9 TWh NG-cell-over-CEMS excess that is not
geo/bio.

The band was **not** moved after seeing the result: the guard's own comment says a miss is "a finding to
report, never a reason to move the band".

**Owner decision owed:** either (a) accept the construction for 2019–21 with a guard rebuilt on the measured
930 basis, or (b) re-derive the fold-in treatment for every year. Option (b) moves the committed 2022–25
artifacts and so touches the keeper.

**Hazard noted:** the derive script rewrites every year it lists on each run. At HEAD it would change the
committed 2022 artifact (219.538 → 219.275 TWh). Whoever extends it should add a `--years` argument first.

## 3. CISO hydro gap

EIA-930 CISO `NG: WAT` is NaN from 2019-10-01 to 2020-08-24, and EIA's Adjusted/Imputed columns are empty as
well. `Net generation` omits hydro over the same hours; the identity holds to ≤3 MW. A new raw source,
`data/raw/caiso-outlook-fuelsource/` (fetcher `scripts/data/fetch_caiso_outlook_fuelsource.py`), provides
`large_hydro + small_hydro`.

**Validation:**
- 2019 overlap: 930 = 0.9965 × Outlook, every month within ±0.5 %, mean \|Δ\| 87 MW.
- 2021: 930 = 1.0033 × Outlook, r 0.991.
- Clock: lag-0 alignment measured on solar (r 0.970), gas (0.986) and nuclear (1.000).

The repair (`src/market_sim/data/eia930/caiso_hydro_backfill.py`, seam `frames._repair_measured_gaps`) fills
only **filed** hours (Net generation present) whose cell is NaN. 11 (2019) and 2 (2020) hours stay NaN, being
DST-ambiguous in the source or loader-inserted gap rows. It adds to `Net generation` only where the filing identity shows hydro is absent.

**Independent corroboration:** in the gap months, `Demand − (NetGen − TI)` falls from +1.7–2.6 TWh/month to
+0.29–0.79, the same level as the normal months. Monthly WAT is now:
- 2019 Oct–Dec: 1.71 / 1.41 / 1.76 TWh
- 2020 Jan–Aug: 1.42 / 0.98 / 0.87 / 1.42 / 1.98 / 1.74 / 1.84 / 1.80 TWh
