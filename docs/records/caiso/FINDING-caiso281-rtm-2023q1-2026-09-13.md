## 4. Representative-day counts, and why RTM is 4.3x DAM

2023-02-15: **RTM 156,791 rows vs DAM 36,081 rows** — but the resource counts are
close (RTM 1,755 vs DAM 1,545). The inflation is **not** sub-hourly granularity:

| | RTM | DAM |
|---|---|---|
| rows | 156,791 | 36,081 |
| distinct `RESOURCEBID_SEQ` | **1,755** | 1,545 |
| distinct `SCHEDULINGCOORDINATOR_SEQ` | 259 | 242 |
| distinct `STARTTIME` | **24** | 24 |
| `STARTTIME..STOPTIME` durations | **60 min, 100% of rows** | 60 min (22,114), 120..1260 min (5,881), **1440 min (8,086)** |

**DAM collapses a bid that is flat across hours into one multi-hour block row**
(up to 1440 min = a whole-day self-schedule; 8,086 such rows). **RTM re-states every
hour separately** — every row is exactly 60 minutes. That block-vs-hour enumeration,
plus RTM's extra `GHG` product (6,861 rows, absent from DAM), is the whole 4.3x.

The column name for the masked resource is **`RESOURCEBID_SEQ`** in both market runs
— the RTM equivalent the prompt asked about is the same column, not a renamed one.

## 5. Granularity — the direct answer

**RTM has no interval/sub-hourly dimension.** Both time axes are hourly:

- `STARTTIME`..`STOPTIME`: **60 minutes on 156,791 of 156,791 rows**, 24 distinct starts.
- `SCH_BID_TIMEINTERVALSTART`..`STOP`: **60 minutes on 136,799 of 136,799 curve rows**,
  24 distinct starts.

The `TIMEINTERVALSTART`/`END` pair (populated only on self-schedule rows, 12.8%) is
also hourly. There is no 15-minute or 5-minute field anywhere in the 28 columns. A
consumer wanting FMM/RTD sub-hourly resolution will **not** find it in `PUB_RTM_GRP`;
this publication is the hourly bid submission, and the DART comparison the charter
contemplates is therefore hour-to-hour with no re-sampling needed on the bid side.
