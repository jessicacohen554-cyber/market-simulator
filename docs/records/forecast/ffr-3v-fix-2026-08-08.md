## 1. The leak, re-derived at this head

`load_renewable_profiles` (`data/renewables.py`) chose the installed-capacity
basis on `is_backcast = config.mode == "backcast"`. A capacity hindcast is
`mode="forecast"` + `hindcast=True`, so it fell through to
`RENEWABLE_INSTALLED_MW` — the canonical **present-day** forward-projection
base — even though `runner.py:1080` had already pointed every EIA-860 loader
at the run's `vintage_<year>/` snapshot. Zone *shares* and the monthly ramp
came from the vintage; only the **total** did not.

Measured at this head (`_eia860_monthly_capacity(iso, fuel, zones, 2020)`
December column on each `data/raw/eia-860/vintage_2020/` sheet, against the
pool `load_renewable_profiles` actually returns for a vintage-2020 hindcast
config):

| ISO | tech | seeded (constant) | vintage-2020 measured | gap |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| ERCOT | wind | 42,000 | 27,541 | **1.53×** |
| ERCOT | solar | 38,000 | 4,864 | **7.81×** |
| CAISO | wind | 6,330 | 5,776 | 1.10× |
| CAISO | solar | 24,920 | 14,615 | **1.71×** |
| PJM | wind | 11,000 | 10,154 | 1.08× |
| PJM | solar | 14,000 | 4,550 | **3.08×** |
| MISO | wind | 32,000 | 26,050 | 1.23× |
| MISO | solar | 7,000 | 2,048 | **3.42×** |
| NYISO | wind | 2,400 | 1,989 | 1.21× |
| NYISO | solar | 1,500 | 664 | **2.26×** |
| NEISO | wind | 1,400 | 1,500 | 0.93× |
| NEISO | solar | 2,700 | 1,519 | 1.78× |

This reproduces FFR-3V §6.1 (which reported MISO solar 2,056 and wind 26,101;
the ~8 MW / ~50 MW differences are the planned-retirement OFF-ramp and the
committed sheet at this head, not a disagreement). **NEISO wind is the
counter-example that matters for rule 14**: the measured value is *higher*
than the constant, so this is not a uniform de-rate.

**Why it is a blocker and not a cosmetic mis-seed.** The pool is the base the
evolution channels ADD to — `runner.py:1707`, `wind_cap[z] += additions` — and
`evolve.py`'s FFR-5E procurement limb reasons explicitly against the old seed
("the base year does not evolve, and its pools are already seeded from
`RENEWABLE_INSTALLED_MW`"). Injecting a measured procurement flow onto a pool
already holding 3.4× the solar that existed double-counts invisibly: the MW
are additive and auditable, the base they land on is not the vintage fleet.
It also compounds FFR-3V's own finding — `_lookahead_reprice_signal` nets the
current year's VRE output out of next year's demand before pricing the stack,
so an over-seeded solar pool depresses the very price signal the solar entry
screen is judged against.

### 1a. A second leg the finding's proposed one-liner would have missed

FFR-3V §7 item 4 proposed "a one-line gate widening
(`mode == "backcast" or config.hindcast`)". That is not sufficient, because
`load_renewable_profiles` is called once with the run's **fixed weather year**
(`ScenarioConfig().weather_year` = 2024 for a plain hindcast), not with the
vintage year. Reading the vintage sheet at `cal_year=2024` puts
`_eia860_monthly_capacity` past `_operable_vintage_year`, which switches on
`_add_proposed_capacity` and grafts the vintage's own 2021–2024 **proposed
pipeline** onto what is supposed to be the base fleet:

| vintage-2020 sheet, ERCOT | read at 2020 | read at 2024 |
|---|---:|---:|
| wind | 27,541 MW | 29,840 MW |
| solar | 4,864 MW | **12,831 MW** |

Those are exactly the post-vintage additions the evolution pipeline is
supposed to decide for itself, so a one-liner would have swapped one
double-count for another. (`_ISO_HOME_STATES` is ERCOT-only, so this leg fires
in ERCOT alone — but ERCOT is the lane FFR-8A-P3 is on.)

A third leg: the intra-year commissioning ramp. Once `monthly` is the vintage
year's array, `vintage_capacity_ramp` (default `True`) would scale the base
pool down through the **vintage year's** COD months — de-rating, in every
solve year, a fleet that was fully in service before the run's first solve
year.

## 2. The fix

`data/renewables.py::load_renewable_profiles`. No new tunable, no new
`ScenarioConfig` field: the switch is the **existing** `hindcast` ×
`eia860_vintage_year` pair, resolved once exactly as `runner.py` resolves the
same pair to arm `set_eia860_vintage`.

```python
hindcast_vintage: int | None = None
if (config.mode != "backcast"
        and getattr(config, "hindcast", False)
        and config.eia860_vintage_year is not None):
    hindcast_vintage = int(config.eia860_vintage_year)
```

Then, per fuel: the seed array is re-read **at the vintage year**
(`_monthly_capacity(fuel, hindcast_vintage)` — a new nested helper that also
carries the NYISO market-solar basis swap, so every caller resolves the same
population), it replaces `monthly` so the base pool's zone split is the
vintage's too, `installed_mw` is its December sum, and the ramp is forced off
for that array.

Rule alignment: **rule 13 [R-MEASURED]** — the vintage sheet is precisely what
a run at that cutoff may know, it is a physical fleet inventory rather than an
outcome, and it regenerates for any vintage (measured for 2018–2024 here) and
responds to changed conditions. **Rule 14 [R-ACCURATE]** — measured beats the
canonical constant, and the seed is adopted in **both** directions: at vintage
2023, MISO solar goes 7,000 → **7,348** and NEISO wind 1,400 → **1,538**.
**Rule 24 [R-REGISTRY]** — no off-registry channel; nothing new to register.
**Rule 28** — no `ScenarioConfig` field added, so no matrix row is owed under
duty (c); this is an input-basis correction, not a mechanism.

### 2a. Gating choice: UNGATED

The charter allows a gate only if one is needed for byte-stability of existing
registered artifacts. It is not, because **the change cannot reach anything
but a capacity hindcast**:

* a **backcast** takes the pre-existing `is_backcast` branch and is not
  touched at all;
* a **plain forecast** has `hindcast=False` and keeps
  `RENEWABLE_INSTALLED_MW` — *even when `eia860_vintage_year` is set*, which
  mirrors `runner.py`, where the vintage switch is armed only for a backcast
  or a hindcast.

Both halves are pinned by test (§3). The only affected artifacts are hindcast
bundles, which are Q.2 evidence expected to be superseded. A gate here would
be a default-off correctness fix — i.e. an armed answer key — which rule 26
[R-DELETE] and rule 14 both point away from.

**Cache:** no `ScenarioConfig` field moves, so **no cache key moves anywhere**
(the pinned default key regression passes unchanged) while hindcast output
does move. That is a same-key invalidation, recorded as **cache epoch
2026-08-08** in `results/cache.py` — the only surface that can see it.

## 4. Measurement — PRE-REGISTRATION

*(Written and committed BEFORE either arm was launched. Whatever §5 reports,
it reports against this.)*

**Design.** A bounded before/after **pair**, MISO (the finding's own ISO;
chosen over ERCOT to stay off the lane FFR-8A-P3 is on), FFR-5A posture —
`--forward-from-base --arm realized --vintage 2020` with every other flag at
its shipped default — window `--start-year 2021 --end-year 2023`. Under the
plain-hindcast window rules that is **solved [2021, 2023], bridged [2022]**:
two solve-years (≤ 5, per charter), the minimum that shows a base seed, a full
evolution step and a scored year. Years sequential inside each invocation
(rule 12). The two arms are separate invocations with separate `--out-dir`s,
run at different code states (the fix is ungated, so the BEFORE arm is the
pre-fix `renewables.py` restored from `HEAD~1`), and registered to
`frontend/data/hindcast/` via `scripts/register_forecast_run.py` — **never**
the backcast registry.

**Pre-registered reads.**

| # | read | pre-registered expectation |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | base 2021 pool, wind / solar MW | BEFORE 32,000 / 7,000; AFTER **26,050 / 2,048** exactly (the vintage-2020 EIA-860 year-end fleet) |
| R2 | annual VRE energy, 2021 and 2023 (TWh) | AFTER **lower**; 2021 ≈ in proportion to the pool ratio, 2023 by that plus whatever evolution built |
| R3 | mean energy price, 2021 and 2023 ($/MWh) | AFTER **higher** — less zero-MC energy on the balance means a costlier unit sets price more often. This is the load-bearing read: it is FFR-3V §6.1's compounding claim, tested. |
| R4 | cumulative evolution additions / retirements by tech | AFTER **more entry and/or less retirement**. Direction only; **no magnitude pre-registered.** |
| R5 | `cache_key=` from each arm's runtime banner | **IDENTICAL** across arms. A difference falsifies "no key moves" and means the arms are not comparable. |
| R6 | bridge contract | both arms `solved [2021, 2023], bridged [2022]`, no `year_2022.parquet` |

**What would falsify the fix** (as opposed to merely surprising me): R1 AFTER
not equal to the measured vintage fleet; R5 keys differing; or any movement in
a backcast or plain-forecast output. R3 or R4 landing the other way is a
**finding to report, not grounds to revert** — rule 1 [R-STRUCT]: the vintage
seed is the structurally correct base whatever it does to a residual, and rule
14 forbids restoring the constant because it flattered something.

**Not claimed by this pair.** It is two solve years in one ISO. It measures
that the seed is corrected and shows the first-order direction of what moves;
it is **not** a re-gate of any registered leg, and no band on any committed
hindcast sidecar is superseded by it.

## 6. THE DELIVERABLE STATEMENT

> **FFR-5E's HINDCAST ARM IS NOW UNBLOCKED.**

The blocker was arithmetic, and it is gone. FFR-5E injects measured procured
VRE rows as a per-year FLOW into `renewable_additions`, which the runner adds
to the pools (`wind_cap[z] += additions`). Against the old seed the base fleet
was the **present-day** constant — for MISO solar, 7,000 MW standing in for the
2,048 MW that existed at the 2020 cutoff — so it **already contained the very
plants the 2021–2025 procurement rows describe**, and every injected MW
double-counted invisibly. Against the vintage seed the base is exactly the
vintage year-end fleet, so a row with `online_year > base_year` names capacity
demonstrably absent from it: the injection is additive, auditable, and
reconcilable against EIA-860. `evolve.py`'s limb comment, which stated the old
premise in so many words, is corrected in this PR.

**Two conditions the FFR-5E session must carry, stated so they are not
discovered late:**

1. **A bounded, quantified UNDER-count remains at the base year.** Capacity
   commissioned *during* the base year (2021) is in neither the vintage-2020
   seed nor the injection, because 2021 does not evolve — **MISO solar
   1,160 MW, MISO wind 2,945 MW, ERCOT solar 3,958 MW, ERCOT wind 4,424 MW**
   (vintage-2021 year-end minus vintage-2020 year-end). This is the same
   under-count `evolve.py`'s comment already declared, now measured. It is the
   SAFE direction and it is ~4× smaller than the over-count it replaces (MISO
   solar: −1,160 MW against the old **+4,952 MW**), but it is not zero, and a
   procurement-channel score should state it rather than absorb it.
2. **Every cached hindcast bundle is pre-epoch.** No FFR-5E arm may be compared
   against a control solved before this commit — same key, different output
   (§5.1, R5). Solve both arms at or after this head.

**Not unblocked by this PR:** FFR-5E's own mechanism
(`vre_procurement_additions_enabled`) is untouched and stays default-off; it
was off in both arms here, so nothing in §5 tests it. What is removed is the
*precondition* its design doc named — nothing more.
