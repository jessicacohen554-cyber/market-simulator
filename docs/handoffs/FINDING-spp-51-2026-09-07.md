## 4. What the verdict means, and what it does not

* **It is a verdict on the spread-clearing form of the P2/P3 seams as a BACKCAST mechanism for SPP**, not on the
  measured `hr_by_year` values (which are correct, rule-13 inputs and stay registered), and not on the ERCOT DC
  ties, whose flow DOES track the spread weakly (0.56–0.61).
* **It does not re-open MISO's side** (rule 25): MISO's keeper carries its own hourly-anchored SPP ladder, read here
  only as the precedent that reached the same conclusion.
* **Forward lane:** in a forecast year no measured schedule exists, so the served schedule is unavailable and a
  priced seam of SOME form is the only forward object. That form is not "clear on the hourly spread": the measured
  SPP↔MISO seam is a schedule with a spread-blind hourly shape. The forward-lane cell stays `U` pending a charter
  that starts from that fact (R-b).
