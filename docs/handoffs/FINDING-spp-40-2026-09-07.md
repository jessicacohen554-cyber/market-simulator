N↔S link (SPP-53) is **live**: it sits at its bound in **1,776 of 8,760 hours (20.3 %)**, so the

**What the kill says structurally, and it is the same object seen three ways:** the two-bubble SPP

## 3. The structural reading (rule 1 — the object, not the residual)

Three measurements, one object:

1. **Direction tie (leg A).** The link binds S→N overnight on South wind + South CC. The real
   corridor is N→S-dominant because South wind is trapped **inside** Oklahoma / the Panhandle —
   `oklahoma_internal` is the market's most-binding group in all three years, ranked above the
   corridor itself (FINDING-spp-14 §5, ruling P1 → **SPP-57 first**).
2. **17× under-separation (leg B/D).** With both zones on the same measured gas ladder, the zonal
   duals differ by pennies even when the link binds ($6 mean N→S dual; the market's hub spread is
   $17 mean, $41 p90). The published hubs are a Nebraska-vs-central-Oklahoma two-point spread
   (PRECOMMIT §1), so part of the $17 is intra-South congestion the two-bubble model cannot hold
   by construction — the same limitation, stated at the gate rather than absorbed.
3. **7 vs 1,172 negative hours; 0.0 % re-curtailment.** No pocket ⇒ nothing to trap ⇒ nothing to
   spill ⇒ nothing prices negative. The wind gross-up (SPP-32's reference rate) is delivered in
   full: 121.0 TWh vs 108.5 measured, +11.5 %, the C1 `wind` miss the full span would have carried.

Secondary, independent of the pocket: the **gas split** (CT 1.78× / CC 0.85× / ST 0.78×) with coal
under by 4 TWh — the energy-only, neutral-band merit order. Coal self-commitment in the North
(the SPP MMU's standing "must-run" observation) and CT commitment physics are the candidate
objects; both are lever questions (rule 19: enumerate what floors the class before adding one).

**Nothing here licenses a band, a floor, an adder, or a TTC move.** The N↔S value stays 3,400
(SPP-53's construction, rule 14); its identification width (2,645–11,121) is O-2's question and this
screen is the first structural evidence on it: the link is *live* at 3,400 (20 % of hours) — the
"pooled value vs one end of the range" question is not answered by a screen whose direction tie is
driven by what the bubbles contain, not by the pipe's width.
