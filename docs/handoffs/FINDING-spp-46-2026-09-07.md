## 2. The structural reading (rule 1 — the object, not the residual)

1. **The LP is right about the fleet it is told the truth about.** CT and ST_GAS dispatch sit on their own marginal
   costs in every hour (0 / 0 / 0 ST_GAS hours and 572 / 287 / 211 CT hours outside the in-merit band); the 110 clean
   CT plants reproduce to −0.24 TWh in 2024 (−0.73 in 2025). The 2024 CT over-run is not a merit-order design
   question — it is 7.4 TWh of CT dispatched strictly in merit in hours where the zonal price is below the CC class's
   median marginal cost, because 1.9 GW of CT rows are priced at $5–12/MWh and a 763 MW plant carries a heat rate
   below any thermodynamic bound.
2. **Both tails of the EIA-923 seam are the same misalignment.** The cheap tail (Elk $0.16, Mustang 4 $0.11, Mustang
   CC negative) is reporting; the dear tail (Riverside $71.66 on 2,750 MMBtu, Muskogee $48.30 on 51 k MMBtu) is the
   AVERAGE cost of a small purchase carrying a fixed transport charge — a quantity defined on a different basis than
   the marginal fuel cost the LP prices. Rule 14's own exception ("measured data defined on a different boundary
   than our representation — reconcile, do not guess") is the posture; the reconciliation is a plausibility band
   against the published state series, the SPP-41 unit-slip construction on a second seam.
3. **The heat-rate seam is one plant-level number per plant.** eGRID `PLHTRT` is applied to every unit of a plant, so
   a mixed station prices its combined cycle at the coal-plus-steam blend (Northeastern CC at 12.24 never runs;
   −1.1 / −1.5 TWh) and a simple-cycle plant whose EIA-923 generation is ~3× its CAMPD gross load reads 3.43. The
   CC side already has a physical-ceiling repair (`_egrid_boundary_hr_repairs`, `EGRID_CC_HR_PHYSICAL_CEILING` 11.5);
   the CT side has no floor. The registered `egrid_family_heat_rates` (nyiso-184, zero DOF) is the mixed-plant
   repair and has no SPP artifact.
4. **What is left after the seams is the object R-17 named, and it is smaller than the stamp says.** The clean
   steam cohort under-runs −5.9 TWh in 2024 at plausible inputs ($3.0–3.7 gas, HR 11–13, mc $38–50 against a $20–30
   price surface). The predictor says the repaired surface (+3–4 % level) lifts it only partly. That cohort is the
   vertically-integrated utilities' self-committed steam fleet; the construction that regenerates forward is a
   self-commitment status from SPP's own market record, a market-design build with its own PRECOMMIT — not a floor
   on observed hours (rule 13) and not a band (rule 14).
