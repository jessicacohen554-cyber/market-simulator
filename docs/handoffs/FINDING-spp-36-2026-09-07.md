The small actual-side move is the resolver working as designed: `_coal_class_for` prefers the

**re-attribution inside the coal family, never a change to the family total** — which is the
property that makes this safe to land without a solve.

(The Page-1 recomputation is a *different instrument* from the committed `classFull`, which is
built from CAMPD unit-hours; hence 62.287 here vs 62.643 there in 2023, and larger gaps in
2024/2025 where the EIA-923 vintage is preliminary. Both are reported at full magnitude; neither
was adjusted. The 2023 gap is dominated by 3.178 TWh at plant **6193**, a bench-population coal
plant outside the 30-plant fleet coal set — noted, not acted on.)

### 4.3 The 2.4 MW — a pre-existing tranche guard, itemised and ROUTED (R-9)

Exactly **one** plant loses capacity, and it accounts for the whole −2.4 MW:

| plant | before | after | Δ |
|---|---|---|---|
| 54211 American Crystal Sugar Moorhead | mustrun 2.25 + econlo 1.32 + econhi 1.08 = **4.65 MW** | mustrun 2.25 = **2.25 MW** | **−2.40 MW** |

Cause, stated exactly: on the PRB curve the plant's 2.40 MW econ block is sliced into the six-way
econ ramp, giving **0.40 MW per slice — below `assembly.MIN_TRANCHE_CAPACITY_MW = 0.5`** — so

re-absorbed. The module's own docstring anticipates precisely this failure mode for the ercot-188
*top* refinement and guards it with `_top_refine_ok`; **the base six-way econ ramp carries no such
feasibility precondition.**

This is a **pre-existing, ISO-agnostic** defect in `src/` that the SPP reclass merely exposes — any
ISO's coal plant with an econ block under 3.0 MW on a six-slice curve hits it. It is **0.012 %** of
SPP's 19,846.7 MW coal capacity and 2.2 MW of a 19.8 GW class, so it changes nothing material; it
is reported at full magnitude rather than absorbed. **`src/` is outside this lane's ownership and
was not touched** — routed as **R-9** below.
