## 2. The object, measured

**Plant 6193 — Harrington (Amarillo, TX, SPP-South), three units, 339 / 339 / 340 MW summer.** A coal
station mid coal-to-gas conversion. Its EIA-860 fuel classification moves by vintage:

| EIA-860 vintage | unit 1 | unit 2 | unit 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **2023** | `SUB` (Conventional Steam Coal) | `SUB` | `SUB` |
| **2024** | **`NG` (Natural Gas Steam Turbine)** | `SUB` | `SUB` |

**`spp_fleet_binned.parquet` carries all three units as `fuel_type = gas_st` in EVERY year**, which
matches **neither** vintage (2023: 0 of 3 converted; 2024: 1 of 3). CAMPD shows it burning **coal** on
all three boilers through 2023–2024 (**3.54 / 2.51 TWh gross**), converting in 2025.

**It is a TWO-SIDED defect and both sides are in scope:**

1. **Model side** — the fleet's fuel classification does not track the EIA-860 vintage the run's
   information cutoff selects, so a mid-conversion coal station is dispatched as gas steam.
2. **Bench side** — the benchmark population is built from measured EIA-923/CAMPD and books
   Harrington's **coal** generation under **`ST_GAS`** (`e_ann` **3.44 / 2.23 TWh** in 2023 / 2024).

Provenance: `FINDING-spp-43-2026-09-07.md` §7 (the vintage table and the fleet/bench disagreement),
`FINDING-spp-46-2026-09-07.md` R-4 + §5 row 5, `FINDING-spp-49-2026-09-08.md` R-4,
`FINDING-spp-50-2026-09-08.md` R-1, desk register **R-ax**.

**Interaction with SPP-49's landed gas-price screen, which you must not undo.** The screen correctly
removed Harrington's impossible **$0.32–1.52/MMBtu** own-month "gas" prices. That is right and it stays.
Its consequence is that the model now barely runs Harrington at all, which is *also* right — a
coal-burning steam unit priced as gas is out of merit — while the bench still counts Harrington's coal
burn as **ST_GAS actual**. That is what promoted this from a reported residual to the single failing C1
row (SPP-50 R-1).

  discovered bug in something else — root-cause it, do not bury it back in the input.

  §7 warns that repairing **2023** moves ~3.2 TWh of model energy out of ST_GAS and would *widen* that
