## 4. Cache-practice finding (duty 2b) — REPORTED, not invented

**The key does not move, and this is the documented house case for that.** Measured:
`ScenarioConfig().cache_key()` = `603c2498bf71d21d` after the change — identical to
`PINNED_DEFAULT_CACHE_KEY`; the ERCOT forecast config hashes to the same value.
`cache_key()` hashes `asdict(config)`, and `QUEUE_CAP_PER_TECH_GW` is a module constant, not a
`ScenarioConfig` field.

**The house practice** is documented in the `src/market_sim/results/cache.py` module docstring
("Cache-key staleness (cache-epoch policy)"). The epoch is deliberately **not** a code token; it
is materialized on two human-read surfaces:

* **Key advances** — recorded at `PINNED_DEFAULT_CACHE_KEY` in
  `tests/regression/test_persisted_identity.py` with a dated cause block. **Not this case.**
* **Same-key invalidations** — a behavior change that leaves every key unmoved — recorded in the
  **cache-epoch ledger** in `cache.py`'s docstring, each entry naming its date, cause, exactly
  what is invalidated and what is NOT. **This is this case.**

Followed accordingly: a dated entry **"Epoch 2026-08-14 — D-31 ERCOT solar queue-cap adoption"**
was added at the head of that ledger. The closest precedent is **Epoch 2026-08-03b** (FFR-SC
NYISO demand-anchor re-derive): likewise constants-level, likewise one ISO, likewise no key
movement, same *Invalidated / NOT invalidated* structure.

This is **not** a cache-*key* ledger edit: no `_CACHE_KEY_OPTIONAL_FIELDS` entry, no defaults
ledger entry, no matrix row, no `scenarios.py` touch (rule 28 respected).

**Invalidated:** cached **ERCOT forecast-mode** bundles — plain forecast, `hindcast=True`, and
the T1-X crossover / T1-FF full-forward legs — solved before this commit.

**NOT invalidated:** every other ISO, and **every backcast bundle and every keeper in every
ISO**. Verified, not assumed: the backcast calibration lane never reads this constant —
`scripts/run_calibration.py::run_year` builds each year's fleet with `fleet.build_base_fleet`
and never calls `evolve_fleet` or `capacity_evolution.new_entry`.

### 5.1 The one real consequence the fast tier caught — and what it means

Targeted file runs were clean, but the **full** tier found exactly one new failure:
`test_emerging_tech.py::TestEmergingCapacityEvolution::test_ccus_needs_high_carbon_price`.
It is a genuine behavioral consequence, not a flake.

That test asserts CCS builds at carbon 200 and not at carbon 0, and disables hydrogen and
geothermal so CCS "competes for the shared `gas_cc` queue group against unabated gas alone".
It never neutralized **wind and solar**, which are neither. Measured at $110/MWh flat, carbon
$200, gas $4:

| | wind | solar | thermal built |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| solar cap 5.0 (pre-D-31) | 5,000 MW | 5,000 MW | 2 GW of headroom → **CCS enters** |
| solar cap 8.0 (D-31) | 5,000 MW | **7,000 MW** | **none — budget saturated** |

Wind 5 + solar 8 exceeds the **ISO-total** `QUEUE_CAP_GW["ERCOT"] = 12` GW, so VRE consumes the
entire budget before any thermal candidate is reached. The old assertion was passing on
**incidental queue headroom**, not on the carbon price it names.

**Repaired by stating the isolation the docstring already claims** — the two VRE per-tech caps
are zeroed for the duration of the test (`mock.patch.dict`, scoped, restores the live value), so
CCS competes against unabated gas alone. Verified to turn on the intended mechanism: carbon 0
builds `gas_cc`/`gas_ct`/`nuclear` and no CCS; carbon 200 builds CCS and displaces unabated
`gas_cc`. **The superseded 5.0 is deliberately not re-encoded anywhere.**

**Carry this to the forecast lane — it is the most useful thing this lane learned.** In ERCOT
the binding constraint has shifted from the *per-tech solar* cap to the **ISO-total 12 GW** cap:
at 8.0, wind + solar alone can now exhaust it. Any post-D-31 ERCOT forecast should be read with
that in mind, since thermal entry (CCS included) is now competing for a budget VRE can fill on
its own. Whether the 12 GW ISO-total is itself still current is **out of scope here** — D-31
moved one cell and nothing else — but it is the obvious next question, and it has its own
demonstrated-throughput record to be derived from (2025 ERCOT non-storage COD was 9.66 GW,
RC finding §D4).
