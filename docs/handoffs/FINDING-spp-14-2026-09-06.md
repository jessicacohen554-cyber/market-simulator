## 5. The four-group binding-share table (P1) — served, on recorded membership rules

### 5.1 The rules (this session's judgement, recorded; the GROUPS are the README's and unchanged)

Inputs: every `State == BINDING` row of SPP's own RTBM roll-ups (§5.4 for what those files carry).
The contingent facility's **area tokens** are the prefix before the first `:` (e.g. `OKGE SPS:BORDER
TUCO:345:4:17` → {OKGE, SPS}; `BASE` → none). Precedence, first match wins:

1. `sps_tie` — constraint names **`SPPSPSTIES`** and **`SPSNMTIES`** (SPP's own ITP interface names,
   the ones SPP-13 §4 found NDA-rated); any row whose **monitored facility is the Potter County
   interchange** (`XFMR POTTER_S - POTTER_S` — the SPS 345/230 kV tie station the 2022 20-Year
   Assessment names for *"north-south flow from this area into southern/central Texas"*); any row
   whose contingency tokens contain `SPS` **and** a non-SPS East area (e.g. `OKGE SPS`, `CSWS SPS`).
2. `oklahoma_internal` — the two named flowgates (**`OSAGE_OG - WEBBTAP4`**, **`RUSSETT - SBROWN`**,
   whatever their constraint id — `OSAWEBCLESOO`, `TMP159_24149` / `TMP128_29363` / `TMP563_26603`);
   any row whose tokens ⊆ {OKGE, WFEC, GRDA, CSWS} with at least one of {OKGE, WFEC, GRDA} (CSWS —
   AEP West / PSO — spans OK/TX/AR/LA and is admitted only beside an Oklahoma-only utility). This
   captures the Tulsa (`CLEVLND7 – CLEV_AEC`, `CSWS GRDA:TULSA_NO`) and OKC (`CIMARRON`, `MINCO`)
   flowgates the ASOM 2024 top-ten names.
3. `n_s_corridor` — any row whose tokens contain a **Kansas-corridor area** {WR, SECI, KCPL, MPS,
   KACY}: the areas that lie between the Nebraska North hub and the Oklahoma South hub (§4). Rows
   with a West token (WACM, WAUW, PSCO, BHBA, PRPA, …) are `other` before this rule.
4. `other` — everything else, **reported by sub-line** (SPS-internal = SPS-only contingency;
   Nebraska / Dakotas; the M2M seams; Arkansas–Louisiana–Missouri; and the `BASE`/unparsed rows).

Share = hours (UTC hour of `GMTIntervalEnd`) with ≥ 1 binding row in the group ÷ 8760 (`derived=yes`;
the year-hours variant differs only in 2024, shown). Shadow price = mean of `|Shadow Price|` over the
group's binding rows (SPP publishes negative values; interval-weighted, `derived=yes`). Groups
overlap by construction (a Kansas flowgate and an Oklahoma flowgate can bind in the same hour), so
shares do not sum to 1. Instrument: `docs/handoffs/spp14/groups.py`; records `four_group_table.csv`,
`four_group_detail.csv`, `census.csv` (every distinct constraint × monitored × contingent with hours,
mean/max shadow price).
