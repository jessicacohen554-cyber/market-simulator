## 1. STOP conditions and the registries (charter step 1)

Both STOP limbs were evaluated in the PRECOMMIT §0 and neither fired: the portal serves every
`RTBM-DAILY-BC-YYYYMMDD.csv` of 2026 anonymously, and neither `Flowgates.csv` nor
`Temp_Flowgate.csv` carries a corridor rating — **no SWPP-owned flowgate spans the North↔South cut**
except `SPPSPSTIES` (the SPS tie, `sps_tie` under the fixed rules), and no SWPP flowgate is a
multi-element North↔South interface (PRECOMMIT §1.2, full census). The corridor SPP prices is a set
of single elements — 115–345 kV lines and 161/69–345/161 kV transformers inside the Kansas /
western-Missouri areas (WR, KCPL, MPS, SECI, NPPD↔MPS/SECI) — each under its own contingency, whose
temporary-flowgate ids rotate while the element persists. That is the `N_TO_H` case of
`_ercot_config` ("one of several parallel paths this reduction collapses into a single link"), not
the WESTEX case, so the registries' per-element ratings enter as the **cross-check** of the
archive's limit-at-bind (§3), never as the TTC.

## 2. Leg 2 — ψ_f, the corridor constituents' transfer sensitivity (2023–2025)

Instrument: `scratchpad/psi_regression.py` (spec = PRECOMMIT §2.1, unchanged). Inputs: the landed
2023–2025 RTBM roll-ups (`State == BINDING`, hourly mean |`Shadow Price`| per `Constraint Name` on
the LMP builder's local non-leap 8760 clock — `Interval` is the interval-END label, so hour-beginning
= stamp − 1 min; DST fold hours average their 24 intervals, Feb 29 dropped) and the committed per-hub
RT LMP (`actual_lmp_hourly_zonal_SPP.parquet`). Groups: `docs/handoffs/spp14/groups.py` rules,
copied verbatim — 1,400 distinct constraints bound in 2023–2025, 319 of them `n_s_corridor`.

**Regression:** n = 26,256 hours · k = 134 (133 constraints with ≥ 263 pooled binding hours, all
four groups, + intercept) · R² = 0.170 · intercept 0.61 $/MWh (t = 2.5). OLS, HC1.
