### 2.3 Why the lane proceeded past a literal gate miss — stated, not absorbed

1. The repaired quantity is a **measured input** (EIA-930 `NG: WAT`), not a mechanism: rule 14

   language is written for **arms** (levers whose adoption is the question). Declining the measured hydro
   level over 89 MWh would leave 2025 at 0.02 TWh of hydro — "burying the error back inside an
   inaccurate input", which rule 14 forbids.

   by the zone split: 100 % of the North, 91 % of the South, and the remainder is attributable to a
   different, already-named object. The mechanism did what its own arithmetic (§2.1) says.
3. The owner's direction on this ISO (FINDING-spp-40 §7, verbatim: *"If structural integrity improves
   but gates regress that may still be a keeper"*) is the standing ruling on gate-vs-structure here.
4. The residual hour lies inside a 155-hour **stuck-demand window** (§0, §6 R-9), so its magnitude is
   itself an input-quality question, not a hydro one.

What this does NOT do: it does not re-cut the gate after the fact (the miss stands as written in every
record), it does not tune anything toward the residual, and it does not claim the screen "passed".
