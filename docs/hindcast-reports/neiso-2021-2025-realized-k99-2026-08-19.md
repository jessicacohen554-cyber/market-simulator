### Root-cause note on the retirement FAILs (rules 1 / 11 / 14 — nothing widened, nothing tuned)

The three retirement misses and the I6 invariant FAIL are **one defect seen from two sides**, not four
findings. It is an **over-fire of the economic retirement screen, concentrated in `gas_cc`**, and the
per-fuel table is what identifies it — this is a *misallocation across fuels*, not a uniform
over-retirement:

- `gas_cc` **+3562 %** (5.1 GW model vs 0.139 GW actual) — this single fuel is 86 % of the 5.92 GW
  false-retire and by itself exceeds the entire actual thermal retirement of 3.253 GW.
- `gas_ct` **−100 %** and `oil` **−100 %** — the model retires *none* of the 0.319 GW / 1.208 GW that
  actually left. The screen is not merely too aggressive; it is retiring the **wrong fuel**.
- `coal` **−6 %** (0.791 vs 0.846 GW) is essentially right, and `biomass` (−99 %) is immaterial at
  0.262 GW actual.

That shape is why recall is 2/4 on a **4-of-4 reachable** denominator (D-24): the units the screen does
fire on are not the units that left. I6 is the same event in the time domain — 23.1 % of thermal retired
in the single year 2024.

run executes") does **not** explain it. The over-fire **reproduces under the pipeline rule**, which is
