**Root-cause reading (findings, not fixes — rules 1/11/14):**

1. **The screens still see a scarcity-free price signal in this harness.** The hindcast
   config leaves `scarcity_pricing_enabled` at its False default, so the capacity screens
   price against bare perfect-foresight LP duals (mean ~$25/MWh): no ORDC overlay, no
   reserve-price signal — the exact revenue understatement the Stage-2 fix addresses is
   switched off in this harness. Against those prices the pro-forma margin (which can
   only *raise* revenue vs the old realized-dispatch basis) still leaves the whole coal
   fleet under its 52 $/kW-yr bar, while the accredited floor — correctly counting the
   realized-era wind/solar/storage pools — no longer rescues everything. The 9.1 GW
   false-retire is therefore the floor *release* exposing the scarcity-free revenue
   signal, not a defect of the margin basis; solar entry fails identically (shape-aware
   capture of a ~$25 flat dual curve cannot clear solar's annualized fixed cost, ITC
   included). Recorded follow-up (not done here — it would confound this before/after):
   adopt the ERCOT default forecast scarcity footing (`scarcity_pricing_enabled=True`,
   the grid's `ordc` cell) as the hindcast harness default.
2. **Retirement grain is lumpy after re-aggregation.** The 2024 step retires two whole
   zone-level coal aggregates (4.1 + 3.4 GW) in one year — post-evolution the fleet
   re-collapses into zone bins, so a single eligible bin is a multi-GW cliff. The 2022
   step, still at per-plant grain, retired **1.71 GW vs the 1.53 GW actual** — the
   aggregation grain, not the screen, drives the +534% cumulative overshoot.
3. The 2023/24 CO₂ gap (model ~88-90 Mt vs actual ~191) is unchanged from BEFORE —
   dispatch-side error dominates both runs; the fleet-path change only moves 2025.
