## §2 fixed-payment pattern — does it reproduce? (P-3D)

Extends the `{ercot,pjm}-…-realized-p2c-2026-07-12` re-score to NYISO. Tested against the
`forecasting-entry-exit-assessment.md §2` prediction: in a capacity-market ISO the flat
capacity payment ÷ going-forward cost (GFC) is ≥ 1 for every fossil class, so **fossil exit is
arithmetically impossible**, and **nuclear is the only class below 1.0** — the only exit the
energy/attribute revenue can decide. NYISO's registry ratios (`capacity-revenue-fom-ratio-2026-07-13.md`,
net-CONE 110 $/kW-yr fixed mode):

| class | GFC $/kW-yr | NYISO ratio | ≥ 1 → can't post a loss year |
|---|--:|--:|:--|
| gas_ct | 21.0 | **4.9×** | yes |
| gas_cc | 30.0 | **3.5×** | yes |
| gas_st | 35.0 | **2.9×** | yes |
| oil | 25.0 | **4.0×** | yes |
| coal | 58.5 | **1.7×** | yes (no NYISO coal fleet) |
| nuclear | 130.0 | *0.82×* | **no — only retire-able class** |

**Verdict: the fossil half reproduces; the economic nuclear-inversion does not appear here.**

- **Fossil-exit-impossible — REPRODUCED.** The economic-retirement screen retired **0 MW of
  every fossil class** across 2021→2025 (gas_ct / gas_cc / oil / biomass all −100% vs the 0.48 GW
  NYISO actually retired). No `reason:"economic"` retirement appears in any year's ledger. This is
  the §2 signature: with every fossil ratio 1.7–4.9× ≥ 1, the capacity payment alone clears GFC,
  so no fossil unit can register a loss year whatever the energy market does. Same direction as PJM
  (thermal retirements −63 %), sharper here because NYISO's real fossil exits are tiny.
- **Economic nuclear-inversion — NOT observed.** The one nuclear retirement — **Indian Point 3
  (`8907_3`, 1036 MW), reason `"known"`, applied at the 2022 bridge step** — is an *announced*-channel
  event honoring the unit's 2020-vintage EIA-860 Planned Retirement Year (2021), **not** the economic
  screen. It is a *correct* retirement (Indian Point 3 shut 2021-04-30) that scores as 100 %
  false-retire **only because the committed EIA-860 retired sheet omits plant 8907** entirely (see
  caveats). The economic screen left every surviving NYISO reactor (Nine Mile 1/2, FitzPatrick,
  Ginna ≈ 3.4 GW) in place — so the sub-1.0 nuclear ratio did *not* trigger a false economic exit.
  This is the same mechanism as PJM's Byron/Dresden nuclear false-retire (announced channel, 2021
  vintage date), but the sign is opposite: PJM's honored a date that CEJA later reversed (genuinely
  false); NYISO's honored a date that reality kept (genuinely correct, mis-scored by the data gap).

## Ranked misses & mechanism hypotheses (findings only — rules 1/11/14, no tuning)

1. **gas_ct additions +5494 % (4.0 vs 0.07 GW) and gas_cc +3.0 GW (vs 0)** — largest additions
   miss. Driven by the **reserve-margin adequacy backstop** (`capacity.py::resolve_reserve_margin_build_enabled`,
   default-on in capacity-market ISOs) + economic new-entry: the log shows a 4000 MW gas_ct backstop
   built at the 2022 step, then economic gas_cc blocks in 2023/2024. The backstop over-fires because
   the seed-year reserve margin is near-zero (2021 RM 3.4 %, I7 firm-capacity shortfall) and the
   one-pass loop over-corrects — RM balloons to 24 % (2023) → 34 % (2024), the same de-firm→overshoot
   the corridor/full-horizon work flags (I12 WARN).
2. **wind +349 % (4.0 vs 0.89 GW) and solar +264 % (8.0 vs 2.2 GW)** — NYISO **over-builds** VRE,
   the *opposite* of the ERCOT/PJM solar = 0 miss. Economic renewable entry sites large chunky
   blocks (1000 MW wind + 2000 MW solar per year, all `Upstate_West`); with no capacity-revenue term
   for VRE the driver is the energy-margin + RPS constraint, and the block granularity + one-pass
   entry overshoots the modest real NYISO buildout. Mechanism: `capacity.py` economic-entry sizing /
   renewable-build pace, not a retirement screen.
3. **thermal false-retire 1.036 GW nuclear (100 % of model)** — entirely the Indian Point 3
   announced-channel event above; a scoring artifact of the actuals data gap, **not** an
   over-retirement. `plant_recall`/economic-screen root-cause: none (screen retired nothing).
4. **storage −100 % (0 vs 0.184 GW)** — model builds no storage in NYISO over the window; the value
   stack + ELCC saturation derate does not clear entry against NYISO's small real 184 MW. Minor in GW
   terms but a directional miss (`storage.py` value stack).
5. **CO2 −26 % → −37 % (2025: 21.1 vs 33.3 Mt)** — model under-emits, compounding **fleet error**
   (over-built wind/solar/gas displacing the real gas fleet) with the known NYISO **dispatch error**
   (the keeper/D-7 gap on the true fleet). Decomposition needs the keeper CO2 gap threaded in (plan
   §1.4); absent it this is the *total* hindcast error. Direction is consistent with the over-build.

## Data & scoring caveats

- **Indian Point absent from the scoring target.** The committed "latest" EIA-860
  `eia860_generator_retired_and_canceled` sheet does **not** contain plant 8907 (Indian Point) in any
  sheet — so `capacity_actuals_nyiso.csv` carries **0 GW nuclear retired**, though Indian Point 2
  (2020, out of window) and 3 (2021-04, 1040 MW, in window) really shut. This is a coverage gap in
  the committed snapshot (same basis as the ERCOT/PJM builds), not a build error; it is why the
  model's correct Indian Point 3 retirement scores as false. MISO's Palisades (2022) is missing for
  the same reason. Flagged, never used to tune.
- **CO2 reference is a facility-exact BA crosswalk, not a state sum.** NYISO ≈ NY but carries a few
  NJ/PJM-border plants, so `score_capacity_hindcast.py` sums CAMPD `co2Mass` over the ORISPL/plant
  codes EIA-860 maps to the NYIS balancing authority (rule 11 — avoid a boundary-misaligned input),
  not a NY-state sum. (The auto-generated CO2 note's "ERCOT≈TX" boilerplate does not apply to NYISO.)
- **2022 timing is a bridge artifact.** Indian Point 3's model retirement lands at the 2022 evolution
  step (evolved-not-solved, rule 22) vs the real 2021-04 date — within the ±1 yr the plan flags for
  events near the bridge.
- `#`-in-generator-ID fix: one NYISO addition (`63625_HJD#1`, 0.8 MW oil) truncated the CSV under
