## Root-cause reading (findings, not fixes — rules 1/11/14)

1. **The scarcity footing is inert for PJM by design and by regime.** By design:
   no ORDC overlay (capacity-market ISO). By regime: even if one existed it would
   be inert — the perfect-foresight LP on the ample 2020-vintage fleet is
   structurally long, energy prices peak at **max $40/$35/$50 per MWh** (2023/24/25)
   with **zero hours above $100** in any scored year. The PJM screens see capacity
   revenue (net-CONE × UCAP) plus a scarcity-free ~$27 energy curve — the same
   long-system artifact the ERCOT s3 run documents. The footing change validates
   the harness posture (screens see the forecast's price formation) without moving
   any PJM number.

2. **Shape-aware VRE entry swings PJM solar from over-build to zero.** Pre-fix
   PJM entered **17.5 GW** solar on a flat annual-mean price (shape-blind, a +34%
   over-build vs the 13.1 GW actual). HEAD's shape-aware capture prices solar
   against its own build-zone hourly CF × zonal prices; against a scarcity-free
   ~$27 flat curve, solar's capture rate cannot clear its annualized fixed cost
   (ITC included), so entry collapses to **0** — a −100% miss. Same failure mode
   as ERCOT: the flat mean was reaching a roughly-right build through an unreal
   mechanism (rule 1), and correcting the mechanism exposes that the
   *perfect-foresight hindcast price signal* — not the entry math — is what starves
   VRE entry here. In the forward market (grown demand, tighter fleet, real
   scarcity hours) solar capture is materially higher; the hindcast's long system
   is the confound.

3. **The retirement screen retires the wrong 4.1 GW.** The model economically
   retires **4.097 GW of nuclear** (0/17 recall, 99.8% false) while catching
   **none** of the 6.9 GW coal / 3.5 GW gas_ct PJM actually retired. Nuclear
   retires because at a flat ~$27 energy price its $130/kW-yr FOM is uncovered even
   with capacity revenue — again a symptom of the scarcity-free hindcast price
   signal, not of the retirement arithmetic. The announced-channel reversal
   supersession (Byron/Dresden CEJA, PR #1432) governs *announced-date*
   retirements; it does not touch this *economic* nuclear retirement. **Report-only**
   — the fix belongs in the capacity/dispatch layer (quiet this wave; solve core
   refactor in a parallel session), recorded as a standing PJM-hindcast finding.
