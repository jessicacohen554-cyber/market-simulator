## 2. The defect — a fact about PJM's own coal meter

`arrays.py::_compose_min_gen_floors` places a coal plant's synchronization floor in `load_rank[:k]`
— the top `k` **individual hours** by the window series. A coal plant's synchronization is a
**whole-operating-day decision**. Measured at zero LP on PJM's own CAMPD record
(`scripts/probes/pjm_h16_coalgrain_phase0.py`; 29 covered plants × 6 years = **173 plant-years, 152
reachable**), never off a residual:

| statistic, 152 reachable plant-years | min | median | max |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `p2m_online` — peak-to-mean of P(online \| hour-of-day), the PLANT's own meter | 1.0001 | **1.0091** | 1.3097 |
| `na_online` — overnight(00–05) ÷ afternoon(14–19) on-share | 0.7876 | **0.9968** | 1.0270 |
| `p2m_hourwin` — the same statistic for the INCUMBENT window | 1.0132 | **1.3011** | 4.8608 |
| `p2m_daywin` — the same statistic for a whole-day window | 1.0000 | **1.0000** | 1.0000 |

`p2m_online ≤ 1.10` on **143 of 152**; `na_online ∈ [0.9, 1.1]` on **145 of 152**. **When a PJM coal
unit is synchronized it runs through the overnight trough** — and **the incumbent window is more
peaked than the plant on 152 of 152 plant-years.** Rule 17 `[R-FLOOR-WINDOW]` clause (b) fails in
both directions: the floor binds at the daily peak and is absent overnight **on the same committed
day**.

**Rule 18 `[R-PHYSICS]`, the sharpest number in the lane:**

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| metered coal starts, fleet | 238 | 290 | 303 | 302 | 302 | 334 |

Worst single plant-year: **1,299 MW plant 6264 in 2024 — 253 implied starts against 4 measured.**
A 1,299 MW coal boiler cannot start 253 times a year.

**The sharp test, actuals only.** In the hours the incumbent window HOLDS the floor but a day window
RELEASES it, the real plants average **267.9 MW** and are online **52.17 %** of the time; in the
hours a day window HOLDS and the incumbent RELEASES, **297.2 MW** and **67.13 %** — **+29.4 MW and
+15.0 points, in all six years**, with the day-only set richer in online frequency on 113 of 152
plant-years. 89.3 % of the window's hours are shared, so this is a statement about the ~10.7 % that
move.
