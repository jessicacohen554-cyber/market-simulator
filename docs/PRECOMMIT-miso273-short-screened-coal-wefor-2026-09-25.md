### 2.1 The defect (rule 19)

The statistical forced-outage rate (WEFOR) models **every** forced outage, short ones included. For coal, the
keeper also carries two measured families on top of it:
- the ≥ 5-day unit overlay (`-unitroute-`), applied to all coal;
- the < 5-day short family (`unit_outage_short_windows`), applied only to unit-years that pass the baseload
  guard (when-operable CF ≥ `SHORT_BASELOAD_CF` = 0.55).

On a screened unit, both its long and its short forced stops are therefore measured. The full statistical term
on top of them counts them a second time. This is the coal twin of the gas stack miso-271 retired.

An unscreened (cycling) coal unit has no measured short family, so it keeps the full statistical term. The
construction has to be **per unit-year**, and the committed extract cannot supply that: a screened unit with zero
events is invisible in it.

### 2.3 Identification: the frozen caiso-187 formula on MISO's own screened fleet

Per year, over the screened capacity only:
- `W_s` = what the statistical term removes, i.e. share-weighted fleet-only (`noWc − B`).
- `X_s` = what the measured windows remove on the screened (facility, unit) set: the ≥ 5-day unitroute, short
  and maxgen extracts, clipped to the year. This is an upper bound, as in caiso-187 and miso-271.
- `residual = max(0, W_s − X_s) / (screened MW × 8760)`.

| year | W_s TWh | X_s TWh: ≥5d / short / maxgen | residual |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| 2019 | 25.04 | 105.18 / 5.97 / 0.00 | **0** |
| 2020 | 20.64 | 125.31 / 4.56 / 0.00 | **0** |
| 2021 | 25.31 | 109.90 / 6.50 / 0.02 | **0** |
| 2022 | 23.54 | 87.13 / 6.69 / 0.00 | **0** |
| 2023 | 18.73 | 106.40 / 5.10 / 0.04 | **0** |
| 2024 | 19.41 | 91.24 / 3.53 / 0.01 | **0** |
| 2025 | 23.86 | 90.56 / 5.18 / 0.16 | **0** |

The residual is 0 in every year, so the keeper's own `wefor_residual = 0.0` is the identified value on the
screened share. No new number is introduced.

Stated rather than hidden: X includes planned maintenance. Coal's statistical POF is already dropped
(`coal_drop_pof`), so maintenance lives in X only. The margin (X ≥ 3.5 × W in every year) does not depend on that.

### 2.4 The mechanism, `wefor_residual_short_screened_coal` (new, default off)

For each coal bin: `s` = the screened units' extract MW / the dispatched bin's pmax, clipped at 1. This is the short
overlay's own numerator and denominator (`outages.short_screened_coal_shares`, miso-266 roster). Then:

`wefor_eff = (1 − s)·wefor + s·min(wefor, wefor_residual)`

- `s = 0` is the identity; `s = 1` is the miso-271 gas relief.
- It is applied in `_availability_matrix` beside `wefor_residual_groups`.
- Coal's summer WEFOR is already off under `coal_drop_pof`, so the relief acts in non-summer hours only.
- **Zero free parameters.**
- **Fail-closed:** it needs `outage_source="historic"`, `wefor_residual` set, `unit_outage_short_windows` and
  `unit_outage_dispatched_bin_denominator`.
- **Scope:** non-ERCOT only, and inert in every forecast run (historic only).
- **Tests:** `tests/unit/data/test_short_screened_coal_wefor.py`.
- **Matrix row:** added in every ISO shard (MISO `O`, others `U`).
