It is not an average

The denominator of every number above is the emissions dual — r_B' B⁻¹, the same triangular solve as the zonal price with the CO2 rate vector in place of the cost vector. It is not the emission rate of the unit whose marginal cost equals the price; at a tranched, reserve-co-optimized vertex that unit is not even well defined. These two views are where the difference is visible — and where wind's and solar's costs come apart inside one market.

What this number is not

Visible, not footnoted. Each of these changes how the figures above should be read.

1 · The dual is one-sided, and this is the DOWN derivative

At a degenerate vertex the derivative genuinely has two sides and HiGHS reports the down one. That is the correct direction for abatement — a new VRE MWh removes net load — but it is not the cost of serving one more MWh, and it should not be quoted as one. Both sides are pinned by tests/unit/model/test_marginal_emission_rate.py.

2 · Zero-carbon and import columns carry rate 0 by design

Imports carry no emissions in this model (import_nodes.py; gap G-E3), so an import-marginal hour reads 0 and understates true system consequence. The zero-share meter above sizes it per grid — it is large in CAISO and NEISO, where imports are 10–30 % of energy.

3 · New-build capex and base CF are NATIONAL

cf_base is 0.38 wind / 0.27 solar in every grid, and capex is one ATB number. Differences between grids come through capture price and expected CF only — never through resource-quality-differentiated capex. A windy grid does not get cheaper turbines here.

4 · LCOE excludes integration, interconnection and transmission

None of those are in the cost. Curtailment enters only through cf_expected being the incumbent fleet's delivered CF — a proxy for a new project's, not a project-specific curtailment estimate.

5 · These are model-SELECTION numbers, not forecast skill

Since rule 22's [R-HOLDOUT] removal there is no certified out-of-sample year anywhere in this program — no year is protected from being iterated against. Nothing on this page is evidence of forecast skill, and it must not be quoted as such.

6 · Two v1 approximations, both stated at the gate

(a) The rate and the price are per zone; the technology shape is ISO-wide. v1 collapses zones load-weighted, so a grid whose VRE sits in one congested corner is scored at its load centre's rate. (b) The capture price is the settled price (overlays in); the rate is raw against the LP's own energy dual. Both are quantified under Method.

Method

Everything here is zero-LP: it is computed from committed run sidecars, never by replaying a solve.

MAC ($/tCO2) = (cost per delivered MWh − energy capture price) / MER_tech cost per delivered MWh = compute_lcoe(tech, year, config) × cf_base / cf_expected energy capture price = Σ(price × tech_shape) / Σ(tech_shape) MER_tech = Σ(MER × tech_shape) / Σ(tech_shape)

Post-IRA vs pre-IRA. They differ by roughly 3×, so the page never shows one without the other. Pre-IRA strips each credit at the layer compute_lcoe applies it: wind adds back wind_ptc_levelized_per_mwh in $/MWh (levelized CRF(life)/CRF(window), not over the full book life); solar re-annualizes from capex_wright_per_kw instead of capex_after_credit_per_kw — the ITC is undone before the capital recovery factor, never as a $/MWh subtraction.

Which price the capture calculation uses — the settled price column, overlays included. A project is paid the settled price, so the revenue side of the comparison uses it. The marginal emission rate is written raw against the LP's own energy dual, deliberately: the ORDC / RTORDPA / DAM-AS adders are post-solve price adders that leave dispatch untouched, so they cannot move a CO2 response. The mismatch is therefore disclosed numerically rather than asserted — the overlay's own $/MWh is carried per grid-year and shown on the tile. The overlay branch is gated on ercot_* flags only, so it is non-zero for ERCOT and exactly 0.00 for every other grid.

The zonal collapse, and its bias. marginal_emission_rate and price are per zone-hour; class_hourly_<year>.parquet is ISO-wide with no zone column, and no committed sidecar carries per-zone VRE. v1 therefore collapses load-weighted before applying the ISO-wide shape. VRE is concentrated in particular zones (ERCOT West/Panhandle, CAISO SP15, MISO North/West); in a congested hour that zone's rate differs from the load centre's and this collapse cannot see it. The direction of the bias is not signable a priori. Routed, not absorbed: the LP already holds per-zone wind_dispatched / solar_dispatched, so a hourly/vre_zone_<year>.parquet sidecar closes it exactly — a v2 item on the solve path.

Inputs (per grid-year, from that grid's designated keeper bundle, pass P1):
· hourly/system_<year>.parquet — per zone-hour price, demand, marginal_emission_rate
· hourly/class_hourly_<year>.parquet — hourly MW by class, incl. the wind/solar pseudo-units (the technology shape)
· run_config_<year>.json — rebuilds the ScenarioConfig for compute_lcoe
· installed wind/solar MW — the one input no sidecar carries, resolved from EIA-860 at the keeper's own vintage and written into the artifact with its provenance. Unresolvable ⇒ the grid-year stays pending, never estimated.
