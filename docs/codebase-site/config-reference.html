Constants Catalogue

Heat Rates (MMBtu/MWh)

Thermal efficiency bins by fuel class and vintage. Lower is better (higher efficiency).

  • Gas CC: H-class 6.3, F-class 6.7, older 7.5
  • Gas CT: Aero 9.0, frame 10.5, older 11.5
  • Coal: Supercritical 8.8, subcritical 10.0, older 10.8
  • Oil/Biomass: Default 13.5

Source: EIA Table 8

Commitment Parameters

Startup costs ($/MW) and min-run/min-down (hours) by fuel class.

  • CC H-class: Startup 63.8, min-run 10h, min-down 8h
  • CT Aero: Startup 12.3, min-run 1h, min-down 1h
  • Gas ST (legacy): Startup 55–75, min-run 12–24h, min-down 8–12h

Source: NREL/SR-5500-55433 (Kumar et al. 2012), OEM specs

Emissions Rates

CO₂, NOx, SO₂ by fuel type and plant-specific CEMS measurements.

  • CO₂: Coal 0.090–0.095 tCO₂/MWh, Gas 0.038–0.042 tCO₂/MWh
  • NOx: Coal 0.003–0.005 ton/MWh, Gas 0.0008–0.0015 ton/MWh
  • Plant-specific: CEMS-measured rates per unit (when use_plant_emission_rates=True)

Source: EIA-923 fuel consumption, CAMPD CEMS, eGRID

Variable Operating & Maintenance (VOM)

$/MWh operating cost by fuel class (before carbon/NOx/SO₂ adders).

  • Gas: 2.0–3.0 $/MWh
  • Coal: 3.5–4.5 $/MWh
  • Nuclear: 10–12 $/MWh (fuel + cask storage)
  • Wind/Solar: 0 $/MWh (marginal cost)

Source: Lazard LCOE 2024, EIA-860 cost surveys

Gas Market

Henry Hub trajectory, basis differentials, and seasonality.

  • Henry Hub Paths: AEO2025 low/mid/high scenarios (2026–2050)
  • Basis Differential: Regional markup/discount (ERCOT +$1.50, CA −$0.50, etc.)
  • Monthly Seasonality: Winter peak ~15%, summer trough ~30% of annual avg

Source: EIA AEO2025, Henry Hub NYMEX futures

Coal Market

Coal pricing by rank (bituminous, PRB, lignite) and escalation.

  • Base Price (2026): ~50–80 $/ton depending on rank
  • Escalation: 1% annual (COAL_PRICE_ESCALATION)
  • By Rank: Bituminous ≈ $65, PRB ≈ $20, lignite ≈ $12 (regional/year-dependent)

Source: Coal market surveys, EIA-923

Carbon Pricing

Federal carbon price paths and state-level programs (CA, RGGI, NY).

  • Federal (RFF): Low/mid/high paths with year-by-year escalation
  • CA Cap-and-Trade (CAISO): Measured allowance prices (2023–2025)
  • RGGI (NEISO): Measured allowance prices (when available)
  • NY CLCPA: Implicit carbon price via RPS/ZERO credit stacking

Source: RFF SCC trajectories, ARB, RGGI, NYISO tariffs

Energy Storage

Storage technology parameters and deployment limits.

  • RTE (round-trip): 4-hour 85%, 8-hour 80%, 12+ hour 75%
  • Cycling Degradation: 0.05% per cycle (when storage_degradation=True)
  • ELCC by Duration: 4h ≈ 60%, 8h ≈ 87%, 12h ≈ 97% of capacity value (STORAGE_ELCC_BY_DURATION)
  • Deployment Ceilings: Per-ISO build caps (e.g., ERCOT 15 GW total)

Source: NREL battery storage review, ISO capacity-market rules

Reserve Margins & Market Design

Planning reserve margin and capacity-market revenue mechanisms by ISO.

  • ERCOT: 13.75% reserve margin, energy-only (no capacity market)
  • CAISO: 15% reserve margin, RA program (~$50–100/kW-yr capacity value)
  • PJM: 17.8% reserve margin, RPM capacity auction
  • MISO: 17.9% reserve margin (ICAP PRMR)
  • NYISO: 24.4% reserve margin, ICAP capacity market
  • NEISO: 15.7% reserve margin, Forward Capacity Market (FCM)

Source: PRM by ISO (constants.PLANNING_RESERVE_MARGIN_BY_ISO), ISO tariffs

RPS Floors

State-mandated renewable/clean generation targets driving capacity evolution.

  • CA SB-100: 100% clean by 2045 (RPS 60% by 2030, 80% by 2040)
  • NY CLCPA: 100% carbon-free by 2040 (RPS 70% by 2030)
  • MA CES: 80% clean by 2050
  • Other states: 20–40% by 2030–2035

Source: STATE_RPS_FLOORS (constants.py), state legislation

New-Entry Economics

LCOE, learning curves, and Wright's-Law deployment scaling.

  • Solar (2026): ~40 $/MWh, learning rate 8% per doubling of cumulative deployment
  • Wind (2026): ~45 $/MWh, learning rate 5%
  • Battery 4-hour (2026): ~180 $/kW, learning rate 12%
  • Gas CC (2026): ~60 $/MWh with carbon price, escalation 1–2% annually

Source: NREL ATB 2024, historical deployment (IRENA, BloombergNEF)

Emerging Technologies

Hydrogen, geothermal, CCS availability and cost parameters.

  • Green Hydrogen (PEM): Available 2035+, ~2–4 $/kg with §45V credit
  • EGS Geothermal: Available 2030+, ~90 $/MWh (Tier 3: pilot projects)
  • CCS Retrofit (Gas-CC): Available 2028+, ~900 $/kW capex, 12% HR penalty
  • Offshore Wind: Available 2030+ (CAISO only in current model), ~140 $/MWh

Source: DOE Hydrogen Roadmap, NREL Geothermal, NETL CCS cost baseline

Path Resolution

Centralized file-path registry via paths.py.

Single source of truth: All file paths resolve through config/paths.py. Never use Path(__file__).parents[...] scattered through the code.

Key Path Constants

REPO_ROOT = parents[3]
DATA_ROOT = $MARKET_SIM_DATA_ROOT or REPO_ROOT
RAW_DATA_DIR = DATA_ROOT / 'data' / 'raw'

# Major source directories
EIA_860_DIR              # EIA generation/retirement pipeline
EIA_930_DIR              # EIA-930 historical profiles
ZONE_DEMAND_DIR          # Per-ISO zonal load files
GAS_PRICES_DIR           # Henry Hub + basis differentials
PLANT_REGISTRY_CSV       # Master plant taxonomy
CAMPD_BINS_CSV           # CAMPD per-plant binning assignments

# Derived directories
PROCESSED_DIR            # Curated Parquet outputs (_processed-legacy)
CALIBRATION_DIR          # Validation & backcast source (_validation-source)
CLEAN_DIR                # Forward-mode clean Parquet (when MARKET_SIM_USE_CLEAN=1)
DICTIONARY_DIR           # Data contract: schema/*.schema.yaml + data-dictionary.md

# EIA-860 vintage management
active_eia860_dir()      # Returns the active vintage
set_eia860_vintage(year) # Switch to a specific year's release

Two-level EIA-860 vintage seam: A canonical 2025 Early Release (filtered by COD ramp for backcasts) and optional year-matched vintage_<year>/ releases. Vintage-keyed caches auto-pick up the switch.
