1. What the model is

A nine-region, full-8760, zonal, pure-LP electricity-market dispatch simulator (HiGHS via highspy, direct CSC matrix, no algebraic modelling layer) whose prices are the LP duals on the energy-balance rows. Two passes per year (P0 base-cost, P1 bid-cost; P1 is what is scored), three ISO-exclusive P1-native commitment bridges in place of MIP unit commitment, renewables as bounded decision variables, in-LP reserves with scarcity (ORDC/RCPF) structure, and a deliberately myopic one-pass annual capacity-evolution wrapper over 2026–2050. A backcast mode with a written admissibility rule for measured inputs (rules 13/14) is used to calibrate 2019–2025 against actuals.

Where it sits

Closest in class to a zonal production-cost model with a light long-term wrapper. It is unusual in three ways: in-LP ORDC and reserve co-optimisation at zonal grain with published scarcity overlays; a reproducibility contract (solve-surface-keyed cache, per-run config, DOF ledger, per-year isolated containers) that no surveyed comparator documents; and a public, machine-scored hindcast that names its own failures. It is not a nodal tool (no PTDF, congestion, basis or FTRs), not a co-optimised capacity-expansion model (no joint investment or transmission build), not an adequacy Monte-Carlo engine, and not a validated forecaster (no golden 2026–2050 solve; the forecast program is at proof-of-concept for every ISO).

Validation norms

In August 2026 the regime rested on a machine-scored rubric, a three-tier fail-closed holdout and pre-registration, and exceeded every surveyed comparator. On 2026-09-09 the holdout machinery was removed by owner instruction (rule 22; rule-history §18). The record states the cost itself: there is no longer a certified out-of-sample number anywhere in the program. On hindcast transparency the model still leads; on out-of-sample it is now at parity with comparators, none of which publish one; pre-registration without a protected year constrains process, not information. Honest placement: in-sample calibration with transparent, pre-registered accounting.

4. Ranked findings

Each item is cited to file and line in the lane report named in brackets. Ranked by consequence for anyone relying on a number from this model.

F1 · No certified out-of-sample number exists. The holdout regime was removed on 2026-09-09 (rule 22). Pre-registration survives, but every year 2019–2025 is iterated against. Any claim of the form "held-out year X scored Y" is no longer permitted by the repo's own record. [C, E]
F2 · Two of nine ISOs are CALIBRATED under the rule the dashboard applies. NEISO and NYISO pass every registered year. The other seven fail on 2019–2021 fuel-mix or dispatch-correlation rungs and/or 2022–2025 price rungs (§2). Keeper-shard prose that still reads "CALIBRATED on the designated 2023–2025 span" describes a superseded rule. [C]
F3 · Relaxed-LP price formation is the largest structural divergence from production-cost and ISO practice. No binaries, no uplift, no convex-hull pricing. The three P1-native bridges and the rule-1 band channel partly absorb the gap; the price-shape failures in the gas-shock years are its footprint. Owner-ruled design choice, unchanged since August. [A]
F4 · An undocumented fourth commitment mechanism. model/lp/rows.py::_build_posture_energy_rows builds a clustered-UC LP relaxation (continuous commitment and start-up columns, min-load, min-up/down rows) behind spp_commitment_posture (default off). It carries a mechanism-matrix row (mechanism-matrix.js id spp_commitment_posture, rule 28 satisfied) but is absent from the spec, CLAUDE.md and the codebase reference, where the three bridges are presented as the complete set. Rule 19 asks that it be enumerated beside them. [A]
F5 · Investment is myopic one-pass on prior-year prices with no transmission expansion. Structurally below every capacity-expansion comparator; the spec calls it a screening model. Forward numbers are equilibrium-free and must be labelled so. The forecast program is at proof-of-concept for every ISO and no forward number is quotable. [B]
F6 · The W0 EIA-860 settlement is the right structural move and the proximate cause of the 2019–2021 regressions. Replacing per-lane fleet censuses with owner-filed vintages is what rules 13/14 require. The merit order no longer absorbs the fleet difference, so the early-year fuel-mix rungs now show the real residual. [C]
F7 · Code-reference docs lag the code. docs/codebase/02 and 03 cite a ~2,400-line dispatch.py (now a 34-line facade), a three-pass P0→P1→P2 sequence, the retire-before-CCS order and a no-op fossil filed-date channel; the code does the opposite in each case. Spec §1.9 says there are no ramp constraints; a default-off ramp builder exists. [A, B]
F8 · Environment-variable solve knobs. MARKET_SIM_HIGHS_LEAN and MARKET_SIM_WARMSTART can change which degenerate dual the solver returns and are not in the solve-surface fingerprint as far as verified. Rule 24 tension. [A]
F9 · Rubric amendments v3.10 → v3.17 landed in five days, each admitting a named failing cell to the caveat ledger. Scores did not move; what is ledgerable did. v3.18 and v3.19 followed on 2026-10-03 (the latter retiring CAISO's price_mean 2021 fail as a reference-coverage caveat). This is the self-certification pattern the August audit flagged (gap O5). Owner ruling 2026-10-03: rule 37 now freezes the rubric between promotions, enforced in CI. [C]
F10 · The attestation schema is not fixed. Keeper bundles carry lane-named blocks and the rule-21 free-parameter ledger (calibration_attestation.json::free_parameters) differs in shape by lane; authorized_price_tuning appears only in NYISO's. One schema, validated at promotion, is the fix. [C]
F11 · Keeper-only retention erased the public rejected-run registry that the August audit called unmatched by any comparator; rejections now survive only as records and mechanism-matrix cells. The CAMPD outage seam (gap O4) remains open. [C]

6. Recommendations

Owner decisions

  1. Decide whether to restore a protected year. Without one, label every calibration claim "in-sample" on the status page and in the forecast program's gate (a).
  2. Rule on the fourth commitment mechanism (F4): document it in the spec beside the three bridges, or delete it under rule 26.
  3. Rule on whether forward (2026–2050) numbers may be quoted at all before the forecast program leaves proof-of-concept and the uncertainty band exists.

Engineering

  1. Rewrite the keeper-shard determination prose for every ISO to the v3.13 all-years reading, or have the dashboard render the computed value only and drop the prose.
  2. Run /sync-docs on docs/codebase/02, 03 and spec §1.9; fix the capacity flowchart step data; extend the rubric version history to 3.17.
  3. Fingerprint or prove inert the environment-variable solve knobs (F8).
  4. Refresh the forecast gate-(a) board against the 2026-10-02 keepers.

Method. Five parallel audit lanes (LP/dispatch/commitment; capacity/policy/forecast; data/calibration/governance; site drift ×2) plus a positioning-matrix refresh, each read-only against code and committed artifacts. Comparator claims draw on public documentation of the named tools and are marked unverified where the auditor could not confirm a current detail. A flattering audit is a failed audit.
