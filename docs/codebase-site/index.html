Inside the Market Simulator

A visual, interactive guide to the LP-based electricity market dispatch model — from raw EIA data and fleet offer curves through the linear program, zonal prices, and multi-year capacity evolution. Narrative pages, interactive visualizations, live dashboards, zero jargon walls.

Model at a glance

6
ISOs modelled — ERCOT, CAISO, PJM, MISO, NYISO, NEISO — sharing one ISO-agnostic LP
8,760
Hours per year — every hour solved, no representative days or reduced time sets
~200k
LP variables per ISO-year — generators, renewables, storage, flows, slack & dump
2026–2050
Forecast horizon — annual capacity evolution loop with Wright’s-Law learning curves
