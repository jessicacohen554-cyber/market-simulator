Offer-Curve Grounding

The thermal offer-curve band shape is defensible, but the band heights were fitted, not validated against the real offer distribution. This page closes that gap: every QSE-submitted offer in ERCOT's 60-Day DAM Disclosure (19.5 M offer points, Nov 2022 – Nov 2025) is inverted into the model's heat-rate-multiplier space and overlaid on the run124 bands the model uses today.

Source: scripts/parse_ercot_dam_offers.py + scripts/analyze_dam_offer_multipliers.py · full writeup docs/ercot-dam-offer-grounding-2026-06.md · multiplier = (offer − VOM) / (base_hr × gas), with class cap-weighted base_hr (CT 10.65, CC 7.16, ST 10.75 MMBtu/MWh), delivery-date Henry Hub + ERCOT basis (−0.50), class VOM.

Snapshot as of run124. Every model band on this page is the run124 offer-curve calibration, compared against the Nov 2022–Nov 2025 60-Day DAM disclosure. The CT econ-ramp re-derivation flagged in the verdict below is gated and has not been applied, so these figures are a fixed reference point — they will not track later runs until this page is regenerated.

The three-part offer → model bands

ERCOT offers are three-part: Startup ($/start) + Min Gen Cost ($/MWh at LSL) + a 10-point energy offer curve that is incremental energy above LSL. Verified example — peaker AEEC_ELK_1: Min Gen $34.58, Startup(cold) $5,902, yet its energy curve sits at $23–28/MWh — right at fuel cost, not the all-in price. Mapping: committed ← Min Gen + amortized Startup; econ_low→econ_high ← the energy-curve points across LSL→HSL; peak ← the top of the curve / the offer-cap price wall ($5,000, masked in normal hours).

The model's rising 1.27 → 2.18 CT econ ramp is an offer markup (startup amortization + scarcity), not the observed energy-curve shape. The markup real ERCOT peakers carry lives in the committed band (startup, ~1.4×) and the scarcity wall ($5,000 cap), not in a rising incremental heat rate — confirming the lmp-decomposition Task-2 premise.

Verdict

KEEP  CC bands

Inside the observed distribution at every band (econ_high model 1.41 vs measured 1.51, dead-on). The body of the price duration curve, which the CC econ ramp sets, matches RT to the dollar. No re-derivation warranted.

RE-DERIVATION TARGET  CT econ ramp

The observed CT energy curve is flat at fuel cost (econ_high p50 ≈ 1.11 every year); the model's rising 1.27 → 2.18 ramp is outside it. A defensible re-shape flattens the econ ramp and moves the markup into committed (startup) + the scarcity wall. Gated: the lmp-decomposition shows a flat CT econ over-runs CT and craters ST_GAS through the −0.25 cross-coupling, so any re-derivation must re-run the offer-curve Jacobian + the universal volume gate and be non-regressing. Committed (1.14) and the peak wall (13.15×) stay.

FLAG, DON'T MOVE  ST_GAS

Observed ST offers sit above the model (min-gen 2.39 vs 0.91), but ST_GAS is the deliberate marginal-swing class and the cross-coupling means raising it cascades into volumes. Flag for the gated re-derivation, not an independent change.
