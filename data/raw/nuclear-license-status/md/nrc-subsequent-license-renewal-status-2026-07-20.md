# NRC Subsequent License Renewal (SLR) — status snapshot

- **Source:** <https://www.nrc.gov/reactors/operating/licensing/renewal/subsequent-license-renewal.html>
- **Accessed:** 2026-07-20 (WebFetch → markdown; living page — this file is the pinned audit snapshot)
- **What it is:** NRC's fleet-wide record of SLR applications (the 60→80-year, second 20-year renewal). Authoritative record of the GRANT and the "entry to SLR period" date; the per-reactor info-finder pages lag in reflecting a grant, so the 80-year expiry recorded in the registry is `entry-to-SLR-period + 20-yr statutory term` from THIS list.
