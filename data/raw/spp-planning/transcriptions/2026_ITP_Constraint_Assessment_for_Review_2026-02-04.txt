2026 ITP Constraint Assessment Posting (East Interconnection) 
 
A revised list of constraints and yearly event files for use in the 2026 ITP Assessment have been posted 
to GlobalScape for stakeholder review. The constraints in this list include: 
 
1) SPP and NERC permanent and temporary flowgates 100 kV and above; 
2) Future and study year specific 100 kV and above constraints identified in the constraint 
assessment process that utilized the market economic model (MEM) dispatch; 
3) Select 69 kV SPP permanent and temporary flowgates, and SPP stakeholder-provided/SPP-
validated 69 kV constraints that are affected by generation dispatch; and 
4) SPP and NERC interfaces 
 
• Action Required 
SPP requests that stakeholders review the posted list of constraints and be prepared to vote on 
the 2026 Constraint Assessment at the next TWG meeting. A delay in approval may delay the 
opening of the DPP window; therefore, it is imperative that feedback be provided in advance of 
the meeting. 
 
• Entities Required to Provide Feedback:  
N/A  
 
• Due Date and Method of Submittal 
Any questions or feedback should be submitted through the SPP Request Management System 
(RMS) using the “Submission” Type ” “Integrated Transmission Planning (ITP)” Issue Subtype 1 and  
“Data Submission” Issue Subtype 2. 
  
• Changes from Last Pass 
1) Incorporated stakeholder feedback 
2) Removed invalid contingency (4 + WRNMEDT4 138 - 81YALTP4 138 [1]) 
3) Removed unnecessary branches monitored in base case 
4) Added constraints from B-GEM analysis 
5) Added 765 kV branches to be monitored in base case 
6) Rating corrections
