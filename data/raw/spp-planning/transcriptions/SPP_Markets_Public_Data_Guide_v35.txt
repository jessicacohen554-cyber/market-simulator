State Estimator Data
Description:  Market Participants and Transmission Operators have requested that SPP make snapshots of the EMS model used in Operations and Real-Time Markets available for download[1]. These snapshots include the items listed below and will be posted after-the-fact.
The Network Model (modeling of generators, lines, transformers, etc.) for the SPP region and surrounding portions of the interconnect. This includes device ratings, characteristics, connectivity, naming conventions, etc. A separate model will be posted for the Eastern Interconnect and Western Interconnect. The near real-time power flows for that snapshot in time (MW output of generators, MW flow on lines, MW flow on transformers, etc.) 
The near real-time switching and outage status of generators, lines, transformers, etc. 
The model has not been solved in an offline power flow

Frequency: State Estimator data will be posted
4 times daily for Reliability use (Posting at 5:00 a.m. /p.m. & 11:00 a.m. /p.m.)
4 times daily for Markets use (Data from 0500hrs, 1100hrs, 1700hrs and 2300hrs two weeks prior) will post at 9:00a.m.

StateEstimator_YYYYMMDDHHmm.raw
