Electricity Market Dispatch Simulator

An LP grid model, 8,760 hours a year

A linear-programming electricity market dispatch simulator covering six ISOs — ERCOT, CAISO, PJM, MISO, NYISO and NEISO — on one ISO-agnostic LP at full hourly resolution. It forecasts 2026–2050 through an annual capacity-evolution loop, and runs in backcast mode against 2023–2025 actuals to calibrate. Prices are the LP’s duals; there is no separate pricing model.
